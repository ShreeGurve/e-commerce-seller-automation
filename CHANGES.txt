THE 13 REPORTS  (Reports in the sidebar)
  Sales & profit   Sales | Profit | Product profitability | Marketplace comparison | COGS
  Marketing        Advertising
  Operations       Returns | Claims | Inventory | Purchases
  Finance & tax    Expenses | Settlements | GST support
  Filters (where they make sense): date range with presets, channel, product, category, and for product profitability a sort:
  highest profit, lowest profit (losses first), highest sales, highest returns, highest ad-cost share.
  Product profitability flags each product: TOP PROFIT (10 best), LOSS, HIGH RETURNS, HIGH AD COST (limits come from Settings).

EXPORTS  (buttons on every report; they respect the filters you set)
  Excel   a real .xlsx: bold frozen header, numbers stay numbers (sum / sort in Excel), totals row, sensible column widths
  CSV     UTF-8 with BOM (Excel shows rupee signs correctly), plain numbers, totals row
  Print   a clean page that opens the print dialog: choose 'Save as PDF' there
  Safety: text that starts with = + - or @ is neutralised in CSV (and stored as plain text in Excel), so a note like
  =HYPERLINK(...) can never run as a formula on whoever opens the file.

DAILY BUSINESS REPORT   Reports > Daily business report (defaults to yesterday, date picker, Previous day, Print / PDF)
  sales, orders, units, cost, fees, advertising, returns and refunds, recovered, expenses, order profit, net profit, margin, ACOS,
  top 10 and bottom 10 products, low / out-of-stock, high-ACOS campaigns, high-return products, claims with deadlines, settlement issues,
  the day's expenses, and the 'what should I do' alerts. (E-mailing it every morning arrives with the scheduler in 8c.)

READ THIS BEFORE RELYING ON THE GST REPORT
  It is a working paper for your accountant, NOT a GST return. Sales exclude cancelled orders; refunds / credit notes and GST on
  marketplace fees are NOT netted; only RECEIVED purchase orders count; the channel filter applies to sales only.

VERIFY
  1 Reports > Sales report: change the date range with a preset. Totals row at the bottom.
  2 Click Excel: open the file. Numbers are numbers, header is bold and frozen.
  3 Click CSV: open in Excel. Rupee-free numbers, TOTAL row.
  4 Click Print / PDF: print dialog opens; choose Save as PDF.
  5 Product profitability: sort by 'Lowest profit': losses first, flagged LOSS.
  6 Daily business report: pick a day with sales. Compare its 'Net profit' with Dashboard for the same single day.
  7 Log in as someone with reports.view but not reports.export: they can open reports, and Excel / CSV return 'Forbidden'.
