THE DASHBOARD ANSWERS 5 QUESTIONS
  1 How much did I sell?       sales, orders, units, change vs the period right before
  2 What did I actually earn?  order profit, and NET profit after expenses and ad spend that no sale could carry
  3 Which products earn most?  top 5
  4 Where am I losing money?   5 lowest-profit products + 'Where the money went' waterfall (revenue - cost - fees - refunds + recovered - ads - expenses)
  5 What should I do today?    real problems with an Open button, worst first:
      claims past / near deadline | sold-out products | below-minimum stock | products that lost money in 30 days |
      open reconciliation issues | keywords that spent money and never sold | returns waiting for inspection |
      imported orders to review | listings scoring under 70
  Only items you are allowed to open are shown. Filters: today, yesterday, 7 / 30 days, this / last month, custom range, channel.
  Charts: sales and orders, profit, ad spend vs sales, refunds, profit by channel. Channel table. 'All numbers' lists every KPI
  (sales, orders, units, revenue, fees, ads, cost, refunds, profit, margin, ROAS, ACOS, TACOS, return rate, cancellation rate).

HOW THE SPEED TABLES STAY TRUE
  Each time an order is created, cancelled, returned, refunded, settled, claimed or ad-costed, that ONE day for that channel is recomputed
  from the real tables. Re-running gives identical numbers. Importing thousands of orders refreshes each day once at the end.
  If you ever doubt them: php artisan summary:rebuild   (or the dashboard button).

LIMITS TO KNOW
  - Shipping cost is not a separate KPI yet (courier costs can be entered as an expense).
  - Return rate = returns created in the period / orders in the period.
  - Scheduled daily refresh and the stock snapshot run automatically only after the scheduler is set up (Phase 8c).
